# food-truck-site

## What changes at each breakpoint

- Phones: Cards are stacked (1 columns), the boxes are stacked and the heading sizes are initial size.

- From md (768px): Cards are side by side (2 columns), boxes are side by side and headings increase in size.

- From lg (992px): Cards are in a row (3 columns), the boxes are side by side and headings are increased in font-size.
